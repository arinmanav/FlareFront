Values And Calculation Details

Complete years have twelve reported, non-partial monthly values. Zeros remain included. Missing or explicitly partial months exclude the whole year; this does not establish complete satellite observing coverage. The mean includes every eligible year, including the selected calendar year. The median is the middle value, averaging the two middle values for an even number of years.

Year-pair correlations match January with January through December with December. Spearman uses average ranks for ties. Scores range from −1 to +1; values near zero indicate little rank or linear association, depending on the coefficient. Constant profiles have undefined correlation. The summary is the median Spearman coefficient over unique year pairs, excluding undefined pairs and self-comparisons. Pairs share years and are not independent replicates.

These comparisons keep sensors separate to avoid the harmonized calendar’s source switch. Processing versions, satellite sampling and cloud cover can still change detection counts. The patterns describe reported hotspots, not distinct fires, climate attribution or validated predictions. Small or mostly zero area records may have little seasonal signal.

    Methods

    These coefficients, validation results and quality totals describe the full county. They do not apply to the selected area. Custom-area results use only raw qualifying observations within the county intersection; source availability is retained.

    What The Calendar Measures

    Qualifying hotspot detections inside the selected county boundary. Type 0 records qualify at MODIS confidence ≥ 30 or Suomi-NPP nominal/high confidence. Exact repeated location/date/time/satellite keys are removed; separate overpasses remain separate observations.

    Missing And Zero Values

    Unavailable source periods stay missing. Zero means no qualifying detections were reported. Point files do not establish complete observing coverage or the absence of fire.

        Methods, Sources And Downloads

        Why These Five Counties

        Lake, Shasta, Siskiyou, Glenn and Mariposa rank first among eligible California counties under a fixed 2016–2019 forward-validation screen. All 58 counties were evaluated; 41 met the activity requirements, and 39 beat both raw VIIRS and a seasonal baseline. Ranking uses count-ratio MAE divided by mean Terra counts, with each year predicted using only earlier years. The 2020–2025 observations were excluded from location selection. This is a selected showcase, not a claim that the same agreement holds everywhere.

        Download All 58 County Scores And Selection Rules

        Comparison Protocol

        Models fit 2013–2019. Forward validation predicts each year from 2016 through 2019 using only earlier years. A replacement must lower pooled mean absolute error by more than 5%, with no more than a 10% increase in the five largest reference months' error. Two alternative fits qualify within training: spatial absolute-error fit for Lake and count absolute-error fit for Shasta. The Fire Calendar keeps count-ratio calibration as its common reference method; every alternative is retained in the comparison download. The 2020–2025 comparison is exploratory: these archive years were already inspected during earlier analysis.

        Spatial Aggregation

        VIIRS detections are grouped by UTC date and acquisition gaps of at most 20 minutes. Within each group, detections occupy nominal 1 km equal-area grid cells. Four fixed grid origins are averaged to reduce boundary dependence, then summed by month and calibrated to Terra. This proxy cannot reproduce MODIS pixels or align MODIS and VIIRS overpass times. Agreement with Terra counts does not establish the number of fires or burned area.

        All Candidates

        The comparison also tests slopes that minimize absolute error, using raw counts or spatial occupancy. Zero source detections remain zero. Every candidate, coefficient, validation fold, monthly value, peak error, bias and annual result is included in the download. Earlier experiments on the original four-county pilot did not establish a consistent improvement. No curves are smoothed and no months are removed to improve agreement.
