    TRANSPARENCY & CONTEXTMethods, Validation & Sources+

    What The Calendar Measures

    Qualifying satellite hotspot detections inside the full Los Angeles County boundary, including offshore components. These records measure reported detections, not wildfire incidents, burned area, severity, or risk.

    Type 0 records qualify at MODIS confidence ≥ 30 or Suomi-NPP nominal/high confidence. Exact repeated location/date/time/satellite keys are removed; separate overpasses remain separate detections.

    Reading Missing And Zero Values

    Unavailable source periods remain missing. Zero means no qualifying detections were reported. Neither zero nor the presence of records establishes complete observing coverage or the absence of fire.
